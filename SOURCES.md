# Sources & licensing

Everything here is original work or a well-known open service. No copyrighted
artwork, characters, or licensed media are used.

## Original asset (yours to keep, modify, relicense)
`assets/banner.svg` is generated from `build_assets.py` — hand-authored vector graphics,
one SMIL animation (a blinking caret), layout, and palette. No third-party illustrations,
stock art, or traced material. Free to edit and reuse however you like.

- **Animation:** SMIL (`<animate>`) — native SVG, no libraries.
- **Palette:** original warm scheme — warm charcoal base (`#100D0A`) with a single
  honey-amber accent (`#F2A65A`), warm off-white text.

## Fonts
No webfonts are embedded or fetched. Text uses system font stacks so it renders everywhere:
- Sans (name): `Segoe UI, system-ui, -apple-system, Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
- Monospace (role / subline): `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, JetBrains Mono, Menlo, Consolas, monospace`

If you ever want the exact faces baked in as outlines: **Chakra Petch** or **Rajdhani**
(both SIL OFL 1.1) for the display name, **JetBrains Mono** (SIL OFL 1.1) for the mono text.

## Badges — shields.io
The three link pills (LinkedIn, Email, GitHub) are generated by **shields.io**, a free
open badge service (CC0), themed to the honey accent via URL parameters. Logos come from
**Simple Icons** (CC0), served through shields. They can be removed without affecting
anything else.
- https://shields.io
- https://simpleicons.org
